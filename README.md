## About me

I study how machine learning can improve **combinatorial optimization algorithms** without sacrificing the mathematical structure and guarantees of classical methods. My Ph.D. research focuses on drone-assisted vehicle routing, generative models for discrete decisions, relaxed decision diagrams, and reinforcement learning for mixed-integer optimization.

Before my Ph.D., I worked on fisheries stock assessment, statistical modelling, and industry data science, including marketing-budget optimization and applied machine-learning systems.

## Selected research

- **AAAI 2026 (peer-reviewed):** *Learning Branching Policies for MILPs with Proximal Policy Optimization* — A. Ben Mhamed, A. Kamal-Idrissi, A. El Fallah-Seghrouchni.
- **Under review at ICLR 2027:** *Learning to Compile Relaxed Decision Diagrams by Optimizing the Bound*.
- **Under review at Transportation Research Part E:** *What Matters in Learned Pipelines for the Traveling Salesman Problem with Drone: Search Objective, Start Diversity, and Learned Priors*.
- **Under review at AAAI 2027:** *Bellman-State Decision Diagrams for the Multi-Destination Integrated Last-Mile Transportation Problem*.
